About Me

I build distributed systems, search platforms, and data infrastructure that teams can depend on at scale. At Stripe, I lead cross-company architecture and execution for mission-critical platforms, from unifying Dashboard search to migrating billions of documents and improving ingestion across hundreds of terabytes of data. My work has delivered multi-million-dollar annual savings, substantial latency reductions, and reusable infrastructure adopted across teams.

I focus on turning complex infrastructure problems into shared platforms: aligning teams on architecture, planning phased rollouts, and building recovery, authorization, and operational reliability into the design. I also mentor engineers and build tools that make on-call analysis reliable and repeatable.
